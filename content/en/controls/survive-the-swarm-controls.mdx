## Standard PC Keyboard & Mouse Bindings

Playing on PC with a keyboard and mouse offers pinpoint accuracy for skill shots and swift navigation through tactical menus. The core movement loop focuses on continuous repositioning while managing unit dashes and squad deployment abilities.

Below is the standard keyboard and mouse layout for navigating and battling across the map:

| Action | Primary Keybinding | Alternate Keybinding | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Up** | W | Up Arrow | Moves squad north |
| **Move Down** | S | Down Arrow | Moves squad south |
| **Move Left** | A | Left Arrow | Moves squad west |
| **Move Right** | D | Right Arrow | Moves squad east |
| **Dash / Dodge** | Spacebar | Right Mouse Button | Quick directional evasion burst |
| **Aim Direction** | Mouse Cursor | N/A | Directs weapon fire and skill trajectories |
| **Primary Skill** | Left Mouse Button | Q | Fires active character ability |
| **Secondary Skill** | E | Mouse Button 4 | Triggers utility or elemental secondary |
| **Squad Ultimate** | R | F | Activates full squad synergy burst |
| **Interact / Build** | E | F | Repairs solar devices or opens reward capsules |
| **Pause / Menu** | Escape | Tab | Opens tactical overview and build sheet |

### Optimizing Keyboard Movement

Because *Survive the Swarm* is a fast-paced bullet-hell roguelite, finger placement can make or break your run. Keeping your hand centered on `WASD` while using your thumb for `Spacebar` (Dash) ensures you never lose directional momentum when evading massive hordes. For players using multi-button gaming mice, mapping your Primary and Secondary abilities to side buttons (`Thumb 1` / `Thumb 2`) frees your movement fingers completely.

Adjusting deadzones and stick response curves in the settings menu helps maintain accurate projectile targeting. Because weapons like Blaze's precision longbow rely on pinpoint accuracy, setting your right stick sensitivity to a balanced medium-high range ensures you can snap between approaching targets without overshooting.

Surviving against the alien swarm requires more than basic movement; you must master ability timing, elemental interactions, and cooldown cycling. When commanding a squad of up to 12 diverse exterminators, understanding how the input cadence works is essential.

```

       ↓

       ↓
```

### 1. The Dash-Cancel Technique

Your dash ability provides crucial invulnerability frames (i-frames) to bypass insect walls. By combining a directional flick with your dash key, you can escape encirclement, instantly turning around to aim back into the horde.

### 2. Squad Elemental Rotations

Each unit in your squad utilizes one of four distinct elements: Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Poison. Coordinating your inputs to apply status effects methodically maximizes your damage output:

| Elemental Affiliation | Primary Tactical Purpose | Optimal Control Execution |

| **Lightning (Chain Burst)** | Clears low-health swarms instantly | Activate on clustered groups after dashing |

## Tactical Tips for Customizing Your Inputs

Every player brings unique reflexes to bullet-hell games. Customizing your **Survive The Swarm controls** to match your playstyle can directly improve your win rate across all difficulty tiers.

### Recommended Keybinding Tweaks for High-Intensity Runs

1. **Rebind Dash to a Dedicated Side Button:** Shifting your evade command to your mouse thumb button eliminates input latency during frantic dodges.
2. **Enable Auto-Aim Toggles When Available:** In survivors-like games, utilizing smart auto-targeting settings allows you to focus purely on squad positioning and dodging telegraphed boss attacks.
3. **Keep Interactive Keys Accessible:** You will frequently construct solar panels and crack reward capsules under heavy fire. Having your interact command mapped to an easy-to-reach button prevents accidental deaths while standing near objectives.
4. **Use Hold-to-Fire Options:** Constantly clicking the primary fire button causes finger fatigue over multiple 15-minute runs. Verify that automatic continuous fire is enabled in your gameplay settings.

## Troubleshooting Input Issues and Controller Glitches

Occasionally, players may encounter input conflicts or unrecognized gamepads. Follow the diagnostic checklist below to resolve common control errors:

| Issue | Potential Cause | Step-by-Step Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Controller Not Detected** | Steam Input override conflict | Enable Steam Input under Game Properties > Controller. |
| **Sticky Aim / Drift** | Stick deadzone too small | Increase inner stick deadzone by 5–10% in system settings. |
| **Laggy Dash Response** | V-Sync or frame pacing issue | Cap framerate at 60/120 FPS and toggle raw mouse input. |
| **Unresponsive UI Navigation**| Menu gamepad focus bug | Use mouse or touch screen temporarily to confirm prompt. |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the default Survive The Swarm controls for PC?
The default PC setup uses `WASD` or Arrow keys for movement, the mouse cursor to aim, Left Click for primary abilities, `Spacebar` or Right Click to dash, and keys like `E`, `Q`, and `R` for interacting, activating special abilities, and triggering squad ultimates.

### Can I play Survive the Swarm with an Xbox or PlayStation controller?
Yes, the game features full controller support. Left Stick controls squad movement, Right Stick directs aiming, triggers handle weapon firing and abilities, and the face buttons manage dashing and environmental interactions.

### How do I configure the best Survive The Swarm controls on Steam Deck?
For the best Steam Deck experience, enable default gamepad controls with gyro assist on trigger pull. Additionally, map the rear grip buttons (L4/R4) to Dash and Interact so you never have to take your thumbs off the analog sticks during intense combat.

### Can I rebind keybindings in Survive the Swarm?
Yes, players can customize keyboard shortcuts and mouse clicks in the in-game settings menu. Controller remapping can also be fine-tuned through Steam's controller configuration layout.
