## Core Controls and Input Bindings Across Platforms

Before diving into specific class kits, having complete command over your character's inputs is crucial. In high-density encounters where hundreds of enemies cluster on screen, missing a single dash or active skill input can instantly wipe your run.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       DEFAULT INPUT SCHEMES                           |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
| Action            | PC (Keyboard/Mouse)| Controller (Xbox/PlayStation)|
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
| Movement          | W / A / S / D      | Left Thumbstick              |
| Primary Attack    | Left Mouse Button  | Right Trigger (RT / R2)      |
| Class Ability 1   | Q / E              | Left Bumper / Right Bumper   |
| Ultimate / Skill  | F or Spacebar      | Y / Triangle or A / Cross    |
| Dash / Dodge Roll | Left Shift / Space | B / Circle                   |
| Interact / Portal | E                  | X / Square                   |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
```

Class abilities dynamically modify these inputs. For instance, holding down your primary attack on channeled classes alters movement speed, while dash skills grant distinct invulnerability frames (I-frames) based on the class archetype.

## In-Depth Class Mechanics, Rotations, and Ability Controls

Unlocking the full potential of the **Survive The Swarm new classes** requires understanding active skill rotations and executing precise control inputs during chaotic swarm waves.

### 1. Pyromancer (AoE Burn Specialist)

The Pyromancer excels at area denial, laying down blankets of fire that incinerate minor enemies before they reach melee range.

*   **Primary Attack (Fireball):** Fires straight projectiles that burst on impact, applying ticking burn damage.
*   **Ability 1 (Flame Wave - Q):** Emits a sweeping cone of fire pushing enemies back.
*   **Ability 2 (Inferno Ring - E):** Summons an expanding ring of flames that traps smaller targets.
*   **Mobility Skill (Flame Dash - Shift):** Dashes forward, leaving a damaging fire trail while granting 0.4 seconds of damage immunity.

**Optimal Rotation:** `Flame Dash (into position) -> Inferno Ring -> Primary Attack spam -> Flame Wave (to disengage)`.

### 2. Dread Knight (Frontline Sustain)

Built for players who want to wade into swarms without fearing instant death. The Dread Knight gains increased defense and life leech as his health drops.

*   **Primary Attack (Cleave):** Wide frontal slash that hits up to 8 targets per swing.
*   **Ability 1 (Abyssal Pull - Q):** Drags distant ranged mobs into melee range.
*   **Ability 2 (Sanguine Strike - E):** Heavy downward slam restoring health based on targets struck.
*   **Mobility Skill (Iron March - Shift):** Unstoppable charge that knocks aside light enemies.

**Optimal Rotation:** `Abyssal Pull -> Primary Attack -> Sanguine Strike -> Iron March (to reposition)`.

### 3. Storm Archer (Precision Kiting)

Designed for players with strong aim mechanics and positioning awareness.

*   **Primary Attack (Lightning Shot):** Fast arrow that chains sparks to nearby targets.
*   **Ability 1 (Arrow Rain - Q):** Calls down a barrage of electric arrows over a targeted circle.
*   **Ability 2 (Overcharge - E):** Buffs attack speed and guarantees critical strikes for 5 seconds.
*   **Mobility Skill (Vault - Shift):** Leaps backward while firing a slowing frost arrow.

## Advanced Survival Strategies: Infinite Dungeons & The Post-Wave Ghosts

One of the defining mechanics in the game is the post-wave risk-reward cycle. Once you eliminate a swarm, ghost enemies begin spawning. Staying longer increases gold multipliers exponentially, but getting eliminated means losing all unbanked earnings.

| Ghost Wave Timer | Gold Multiplier Bonus | Enemy Hazard Level | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0 - 15 Seconds** | +25% Gold | Low (Slow Specters) | Farm remaining elite mobs |
| **16 - 30 Seconds** | +75% Gold | Medium (Phantoms Spawn) | Begin rotating toward portal |
| **31 - 45 Seconds** | +150% Gold | High (Fast Trackers) | Pop mobility skills; enter portal |
| **46+ Seconds** | +300% Gold | Extreme (One-Shot Ghosts)| Immediate extraction mandatory |

### Pro Tips for Mastering New Class Builds

* **Manage Cooldowns on High-Mobility Classes:** Avoid burning your dash purely for travel speed; keep it ready to dodge crowd-control strikes from elite bosses.
* **Coordinate in Co-Op:** When running with friends, pair a Dread Knight or Paladin with a Storm Archer or Pyromancer. Frontline classes group enemies together, allowing ranged classes to unleash massive AoE burst damage.
* **Keep Moving:** Stationary play is the most common cause of early wipes. Master the art of kiting in wide circles while executing your primary attack rotation.

By experimenting with the **Survive The Swarm new classes** and finding the weapon synergy that fits your playstyle, you will easily clear the Gauntlet, bank massive gold rewards, and conquer the Infinite Dungeons.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best class for solo players in Survive The Swarm?
For solo play, the **Dread Knight** and **Pyromancer** rank among the strongest picks. The Dread Knight provides built-in sustain and high base health, making mistakes more forgiving. Meanwhile, the Pyromancer clears entire waves quickly from a safe distance using wide AoE abilities.

### How do I unlock the Survive The Swarm new classes?
New classes and archetypes can be unlocked via the in-game character menu using gold earned from successful Campaign, Gauntlet, and Infinite Dungeon runs. Certain specialized gear and subclass perks also require forging with Mythical Crystals.

### How does weapon synergy evolution work with new classes?
To evolve a weapon, you must upgrade your primary class weapon to its maximum rank and hold its paired secondary passive item. Once both conditions are met, your next upgrade choice will offer the evolved weapon synergy, significantly boosting damage and adding unique combat effects.

### What should I do when the ghosts appear after a wave?
Ghosts appear after a wave is cleared to incentivize high-risk farming. While they are active, your gold multiplier increases over time. However, ghosts deal massive damage and cannot be easily killed. As soon as ghost density increases or mobility cooldowns are spent, head straight into the portal to bank your loot safely.
