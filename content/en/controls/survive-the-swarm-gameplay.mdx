## Core Controls and Input Schemes

Survive the Swarm utilizes a streamlined, fast-paced control setup designed to let players focus on positioning, squad coordination, and dodging dense bullet patterns. The control interface accommodates both traditional keyboard-and-mouse setups and gamepad controllers.

Community feedback from early demo builds highlights that while standard gamepad navigation functions well during real-time combat, menu elements and achievement screens continue to receive UI polish.

| Input Action | Keyboard & Mouse Mapping | Gamepad Mapping (Xbox / Steam Deck) | Tactical Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Movement / Direction** | `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` | Left Analog Stick | Maintain spacing, kite swarms, reposition squad |
| **Dash / Evade** | `Spacebar` | `A` / Cross Button | Burst through tight enemy choke points safely |
| **Primary Squad Fire** | Auto-fire / Left Mouse Click | Right Trigger (`RT`) / Auto | Continuous baseline damage against minor insect waves |
| **Special Character Skill** | `Q` / `E` / Right Mouse Click | `X` / `Y` Buttons | Activate elemental bursts and crowd-control abilities |
| **Squad Ability Trigger** | `1`–`4` Number Keys | Right Bumper (`RB`) / `LB` | Trigger specific squad member cooldown skills |
| **Interact / Build** | `F` | `B` / Circle Button | Construct field structures like solar collectors |
| **Pause / Tactical View** | `Esc` / `Tab` | Start / View Menu | Check squad builds, active modifiers, and stats |

## Essential Combat Tactics and Pro Tips

To consistently clear full runs and earn achievements such as "Upstanding Citizen," players should integrate these tactical habits into their regular runs:

1. **Circle-Kite the Periphery:** Avoid cutting directly through dense insect packs. Always lead the swarm in wide, sweeping arcs across open clearings.
2. **Prioritize Timed Quests:** When solar panel build objectives appear, immediately clear the surrounding perimeter, activate the construct, and defend the perimeter until the reward capsule drops.
3. **Manage High-Clone Abilities Carefully:** Player experiences indicate that stacking too many active illusion or clone abilities (such as Kia's Photopsia skill) can clutter visual clarity. Balance clone-heavy builds with clear elemental AoE markers.
4. **Dash Through Projectiles, Not Along Them:** Use your invulnerability frames during a dash to pass straight through incoming venom spikes rather than running parallel to enemy projectile paths.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes Survive The Swarm gameplay different from other survivors-like games?
Unlike single-character auto-shooters, **Survive The Swarm gameplay** centers around assembling a 4-unit squad mid-run, managing elemental reaction combos (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison), and completing active objectives like constructing solar stations within a structured 15-minute match limit.

### Can you play Survive the Swarm fully with a controller?
Yes. The game features full gamepad support. While some in-game menus and achievement screens may require minor interface navigation adjustments on handheld devices, core combat controls, dashing, and ability triggers respond cleanly on gamepads.

### How do Honor Points work in Survive the Swarm?
Honor Points are the primary meta-progression currency. You earn them during every run regardless of victory or defeat. They are spent between runs on squad-wide permanent stat boosts, including enhanced movement speed, reduced ability cooldowns, and higher base damage output.

### What should I prioritize during the first 5 minutes of a run?
Focus on completing initial quest events, building field structures, and gathering XP to unlock early skill perks. Securing your second squad member via recruitment events early in the run creates the elemental synergy needed to survive mid-game swarms.
