## Tactical Movement: Dodging, Dashing, and Space Control

When thousands of insects converge on your squad, movement discipline separates a failed attempt from a completed run. Incorporating smart movement principles is a cornerstone of **Survive The Swarm how to survive** without taking unnecessary damage.

```
       [ SWARM ADVANCE ]
             |  |  |
             v  v  v
        [ Kiting Arc ]
      /                \
[ Safe Zone ] <--- (Dash) --- [ Squad Position ]
      \                /
        [ Choke Point ]
```

### Top 5 Movement Rules for Swarm Survival

1. **Maintain Circular Kiting Patterns:** Never walk in straight lines into unrevealed map territory. Circle around cleared areas to pull bugs into condensed columns for your area-of-effect (AoE) weapons.
2. **Preserve Your Dash Cooldown:** Treat your dash as an emergency escape mechanism, not a basic locomotion tool. Save it for dodging untelegraphed lunges or dashing through thin perimeter walls.
3. **Avoid Hard Map Edges:** Getting pinned against map boundaries is the most common cause of sudden team wipes. Keep at least half a screen of clearance between your squad and impassable terrain.
4. **Funnel Through Environmental Choke Points:** Guide massive waves between rock formations or obstacles so elemental effects like Ice and Fire deal maximum overlapping damage.
5. **Collect XP Orbs in Sweeping Passes:** Do not dive backward into the center of a swarm to grab experience crystals. Clear a path, rotate around, and gather drops safely on your next loop.

## Essential Tips for Achieving Consistent Clears

To ensure consistent victories, keep this quick checklist in mind during every session:

* **Diversify Elements:** Never stack purely one element. A balanced team with Ice for control, Fire/Lightning for group clearing, and Poison for single-target damage handles all enemy compositions.
* **Prioritize Recruit Events Early:** A secondary squad member effectively doubles your attack vectors. Prioritize recruit beacons over minor XP pickups in the first 4 minutes.
* **Watch Weapon Card Details:** Always inspect stat cards carefully when picking gear upgrades. Look for synergies that boost critical strike chance and attack speed rather than redundant raw stats.
* **Clear Elite Surrounds First:** When fighting an elite bug, focus your area attacks on its escort swarm first so your squad's single-target fire isn't absorbed by low-tier insects.

By following these fundamental guidelines on **Survive The Swarm how to survive**, you will steadily transform your squad from desperate survivors into an unstoppable extermination force.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective squad setup in Survive The Swarm?
A balanced team featuring at least one frontline crowd-control unit (like Isla with Ice affinity) combined with two backline damage dealers (such as Blaze using Fire or Lightning) offers the best balance of safety and DPS. This setup slows aggressive bugs while burning down large clusters before they surround you.

### In Survive The Swarm how to survive when completely surrounded?
If a swarm encloses your squad, do not panic and dash blindly into the dense center. Use your dash ability to punch through the thinnest section of the perimeter wall, then execute a wide kiting arc while firing piercing or area-of-effect abilities back into the pursuing mass.

### Do I lose all progress if my squad dies before the 15-minute mark?
No. *Survive the Swarm* rewards Honor Points based on your performance and enemies defeated, even on failed runs. You can spend these points between attempts in the meta-progression menu to permanently enhance your squad's movement speed, attack damage, and cooldown rates.

### How do I complete all quests in a single run for achievements?
To complete every quest (such as the requirements for achievements like *Upstanding Citizen*), you must actively route your path toward objective markers immediately when they spawn. Focus on building every solar panel within the time limit, destroying marked elite monsters, and retrieving reward capsules as soon as they drop.
