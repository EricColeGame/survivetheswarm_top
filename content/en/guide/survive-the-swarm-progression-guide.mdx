## Core Rules and Early-to-Mid Game Foundations

Before diving into specific gear purchases, establishing basic resource management rules is vital. Many newer players stunt their account progress by prematurely hatching rare eggs or wasting limited crafting components on non-essential equipment.

### Essential Rules to Protect Your Resources

*   **Never Hatch Diamond or Gifted Eggs Directly:** Save Diamond Eggs exclusively for endgame crafting (such as high-tier masks). Avoid placing gifted eggs randomly onto hive slots; save guaranteed star items for specific, planned upgrades.
*   **Maintain Hive Utility Balance:** Throughout the early and mid stages, community reports suggest maintaining a foundation of 4–5 Carpenter Bees for token marks, 3–4 Music Bees for Melody and Focus buffs, and 1–2 Baby Bees for the critical Baby Love luck bonus.
*   **Puffshroom and Planter Efficiency:** Always place planters as soon as you unlock them (Plastic, Blue/Red Clay, Candy, Tacky, and Pesticide). Planters supply steady Nectar buffs and passively harvest rare crafting supplies like Glitter, Wax, and Glue.

| Rule Category | Do This | Avoid This |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Egg Usage** | Save Diamond & Star Eggs for crafting and late SSA targets | Hatching Diamond/Gifted Eggs on basic hive slots |
| **Translator Priority** | 1st to Gifted Riley, 2nd to Bucko, 3rd to Stick Bug | Donating randomly without a specific quest focus |
| **Resource Hoarding** | Save Magic Beans and Star Jellies for Spirit Bear gear | Spasting Star Jellies before Spirit Petal milestones |
| **Puffshroom Runs** | Run with 3–4 players for shared loot scaling | Solo grinding Puffshrooms underleveled |

## Mid-Game Expansion: 40 Bees, Bossing, and Mask Selection

Hitting 40 bees marks the transition into dedicated boss farming and serious badge grinding. At this point, your combat anchor—Vicious Bee—should be leveled aggressively.

### Leveling Benchmarks for Monster Hunting

To take down Coconut Crab without spending half an hour running in circles, level your Vicious Bee directly to Level 12 while keeping the rest of your hive around Level 9–10. A Level 12 Vicious Bee can drop Coconut Crab in under 10 minutes, unlocking top-tier drops and Coconut Field access.

| Hive Milestone | Recommended Bee Level | Primary Monster Target | Expected Fight Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **35 Bees** | Level 8–9 | Mondo Chick / Tunnel Bear | Standard encounter speed |
| **40 Bees** | Level 10 (Vic Bee Lvl 12) | Coconut Crab | 8–10 Minutes |
| **45 Bees** | Level 11–12 | Commando Chick (50 Kills) | Rapid clear cycles |
| **48+ Bees** | Level 14–15+ | Stump Snail AFK / High-tier bosses | Passive background clears |

### Mid-Game Mask Priority

Skip niche early masks like Bubble Mask and hold your resources for the Honey Mask. The Honey Mask provides reliable coin scatters and conversion buffs that carry your honey production through the mid-game. After securing the Honey Mask, begin passively crafting materials for the Gummy Mask.

## Spirit Bear Quests and Spirit Petal Allocation

The Spirit Petals awarded by Spirit Bear represent some of the most critical decision points in the game. Making a mistake here can slow your honey generation for weeks. 

In this **Survive The Swarm progression guide**, we strongly advocate securing your gathering power before gambling on shrine favor.

```
Spirit Petal 1 ──► Petal Wand (Direct gathering & token conversion)
Spirit Petal 2 ──► Petal Belt (Huge capacity, convert rate, and petal storm)
Spirit Petal 3 ──► Wind Shrine (Donate for Windy Bee unlock)
```

### The Spirit Petal Roadmap

1.  **First Petal (Petal Wand):** Craft the Petal Wand immediately. It provides consistent pollen collection and converts field tokens automatically.
2.  **Second Petal (Petal Belt):** Do not donate your second petal to the Wind Shrine. Community data proves that shrine favor is usually too low at this stage, often requiring 200–400 Cloud Vials to obtain Windy Bee. The Petal Belt provides immediate, guaranteed stats.
3.  **Third Petal (Windy Bee):** Once your favor has accumulated naturally through mid-to-late game shrine donations, use the final petal to tame Windy Bee.

| Spirit Petal Item | Required Materials Overview | Priority Rank | Progression Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Petal Wand** | 1 Spirit Petal, 10 Star Jelly, 25 Glitter, 75 Enzymes | 1st | High active farming efficiency |
| **Petal Belt** | 1 Spirit Petal, 25 Star Jelly, 50 Glitter, 100 Glue | 2nd | Massive capacity & capacity buffs |
| **Wind Shrine Donation** | 1 Spirit Petal (Donated for Windy Bee unlock) | 3rd | RNG-dependent late-game utility |

## Late-Game Route: Supreme Star Amulet and Color Specialization

Reaching 45 to 50 bees shifts your focus entirely toward unlocking the Supreme Star Amulet (SSA). You must discover 40 unique Gifted Bee types to purchase the SSA, which permanently unlocks game-changing passive abilities.

As outlined in our **Survive The Swarm progression guide**, the safest and most cost-effective path is transitioning into a **Solo Pop Star Blue Hive** immediately after unlocking SSA.

### Late-Game Gear and Container Priority

*   **Coconut Canister First:** Never buy Coconut Clogs before the Coconut Canister. The Canister boosts boost yields from single-digit billions to well over 10–15 billion honey per session.
*   **Diamond Mask vs. Demon Mask:** If you plan on rolling a Blue Hive (recommended for initial SSA honey farming), prioritize the Diamond Mask.
*   **Sprinkler Upgrades:** Work through Golden Gushers up to the Diamond Sprinkler, aiming for the Supreme Sprinkler once you earn 10 Field Grandmaster badges.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most common mistake to avoid in this Survive The Swarm progression guide?
The most frequent mistake is donating the second Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine instead of crafting the Petal Belt. Donating early often results in wasted Cloud Vials due to low favor, whereas the Petal Belt grants immediate capacity and conversion stats.

### Why should I choose a Blue Hive first upon unlocking the Supreme Star Amulet?
Blue Hives powered by Pop Star are significantly cheaper to build and operate than Red or White hives. They generate consistent honey without demanding perfect double-passive amulets or massive item consumables.

### When should I buy and gift Puppy Bee?
Puppy Bee should be the last event bee you purchase. Its primary value lies in its gifted hive bonus (extra treat bond), meaning it provides negligible utility until you are ready to spend tens of billions of honey leveling bees past Level 12.

### How do I collect enough Star Jellies for the Petal Belt?
Avoid using Star Jellies for hive re-rolls starting from your 4th Spirit Bear quest. Rely on crafting via the Blender, defeating Coconut Crab and Tunnel Bear, and completing periodic event quests to stockpile the 25 Star Jellies required.
