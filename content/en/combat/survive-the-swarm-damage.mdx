## Core Damage Mechanics and Stat Scaling

In *Survive the Swarm*, outputting raw DPS requires a balance between base weapon stats, attack frequency, and modifier multipliers. While base damage provides the floor for your attacks, critical strikes and status procs exponentially raise your damage ceiling.

### Primary Offensive Attributes

Every squad member relies on four core pillars to deliver consistent DPS across a run:

*   **Base Damage:** The raw numeric hit delivered by a weapon or ability before modifiers.
*   **Attack Speed / Cooldown Reduction:** Determines how frequently abilities fire. Faster cooldowns mean higher uptime on crowd-control and burst damage.
*   **Critical Strike Chance & Multiplier:** Increases the likelihood and impact of bonus-damage hits. For instance, precision weapons like the Mitosic Arrow leverage high base critical chance to burst down tough targets.
*   **Area of Effect (AoE) & Piercing:** Dictates how many enemies take damage from a single projectile or swing, which is crucial during dense swarm phases.

| Combat Stat | Primary Benefit | Scaling Priority | Best Suited Playstyles |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Damage** | Increases flat hit numbers | High (Early Game) | Heavy melee, single-target snipers |
| **Attack Speed** | Increases proc rate and volume | High (Mid Game) | Rapid-fire archers, elemental spreaders |
| **Critical Chance** | Multiplies burst potential | Very High (Late Game) | Precision ranged, boss-killer setups |
| **Area of Effect** | Clears dense insect packs | Critical (All Phases) | Frontline brawlers, splash mages |
| **Cooldown Reduction** | Spams tactical squad skills | High (Late Game) | Summoners, support hybrids |

## Elemental Damage Types and Status Effects

The game features 12 diverse exterminators, each tied to specific elemental affinities. Combining elements allows you to exploit enemy vulnerabilities and multiply your overall **Survive The Swarm damage** output.

```
[Fire: DoT & Burn] + [Ice: Slow & Shatter] = Thermal Collapse (Burst AoE)
[Lightning: Chain & Stun] + [Poison: Corrosion] = Toxic Overload (Armor Shred)
```

### Elemental Affinities Breakdown

1.  **Fire:** Excels at delivering sustained damage-over-time (DoT) and bursting tightly clustered groups.
2.  **Ice:** Slows and freezes targets, creating damage windows while setting up shattered critical hits.
3.  **Lightning:** Features chaining projectiles that excel at clearing sweeping waves and interrupting enemy casts.
4.  **Poison:** Applies stacking corrosion that weakens high-health elites and melts boss armor bars over extended engagements.

| Element | Primary Combat Effect | Secondary Synergy | Recommended Target Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fire** | Stacking Burn DoT | Explodes on death | Light swarmers, creeping crawlers |
| **Ice** | Movement & Attack Slow | Increases incoming Crit Damage | Aggressive chargers, fast elites |
| **Lightning** | Arc/Chain electric hits | Stuns small-to-medium bugs | Wide flanking hordes, flying pests |
| **Poison** | Armor Shred / % Health Drain | Spreads clouds upon target demise | Tanky carapace elites, stage bosses |

## Meta-Progression: Spending Honor Points for Persistent Damage

Whether you successfully extract or perish under an insect swarm, every run rewards Honor Points. Investing these points into permanent account upgrades provides the fundamental stats required to tackle higher difficulty tiers.

### Recommended Honor Point Upgrade Path

1.  **Squad Global Damage:** Grants a flat percentage bonus to all recruited units.
2.  **Cooldown Recovery:** Increases ability activation frequency across the entire squad.
3.  **Critical Efficiency:** Boosts both baseline critical hit rate and critical strike multipliers.
4.  **Movement Speed:** Indirectly increases your damage uptime by allowing you to kite safely while firing.

## Advanced Combat Tactics to Maximize DPS

To squeeze every drop of efficiency out of your squad, integrate these tactical habits into your gameplay:

*   **Funneling and Kiting:** Never run straight away from swarms. Circle the perimeter of enemy clusters to compress them into tight choke points, maximizing the coverage of your AoE and piercing weapons.
*   **Target Prioritization:** Use precision units like Blaze to manually target elite disruptors and ranged spitting insects before focusing on standard ground units.
*   **Synchronized Cooldowns:** Time your dash abilities and heavy team attacks when elites are afflicted with Ice or Poison debuffs to multiply burst damage windows.
*   **Solar Panel Defense Timing:** When completing solar panel quest objectives, position heavy melee fighters near the perimeter to peel bugs away while ranged units deal uninterrupted DPS from the center.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to increase Survive The Swarm damage early in a run?
The quickest way to boost your damage early on is to complete the initial timed Quest Events. These grant reward capsules containing weapon evolutions and squad bonuses that significantly outperform basic level-up stats.

### How do elemental status effects interact with base weapon damage?
Elemental effects like Burn and Poison deal damage based on a combination of your unit's elemental potency and base attack stats. Applying Poison shreds enemy armor, which allows all subsequent physical and elemental attacks from your squad to deal increased damage.

### Which stats should I prioritize for single-target boss fights?
For boss encounters, prioritize Critical Strike Chance, Critical Multiplier, and Armor Shred (Poison). High single-target DPS units equipped with precision weapons like longbows or focused rifles melt boss health bars much faster than wide-area splash weapons.

### Do persistent Honor Point upgrades apply to newly recruited mid-run squad members?
Yes. Meta-progression bonuses purchased with Honor Points apply globally across your entire squad, including any exterminators recruited during the match.
