## Combat Movement & Positioning Tactics

Surviving intense horde conditions is largely a game of geometry. Running blindly in a straight line will inevitably corner your character against arena boundaries, leading to instant failure.

### Arena Navigation Strategies

- **Circular Orbiting (The Ring Route)**: Continuously travel along the outer third of the arena map. This forces spawning enemies to trail behind you in a manageable, concentrated train rather than surrounding your perimeter.
- **Micro-Figure-8 Weaving**: When the center of the arena fills with hazard zones, weave in an overlapping figure-8 pattern. This compresses the swarm into a tight crossfire zone for your auto-firing weapons.
- **Corner Funneling Bait**: Approach a corner just enough to pull the outer edge of the swarm, then immediately dash outward into open space before the wave closes in.
