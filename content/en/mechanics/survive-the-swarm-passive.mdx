## Core Passive Mechanics: Triggers, Stacks, and Cooldowns

Passive abilities operate through dedicated background counters displayed near your main interface hotbar. Whenever you collect specific tokens—such as Boosts, Marks, or Combat tokens—the corresponding passive icon increments until reaching its activation threshold. Once triggered, the counter resets back to zero.

Many advanced defensive and conversion effects enforce internal cooldowns. While on cooldown, the icon displays a red countdown timer during which no actions will advance the trigger counter. Furthermore, swapping gear in an attempt to bypass cooldown timers triggers an initial lock, enforcing tactical planning.

```
Trigger Event (Tokens / Consumables) ──> Threshold Reached ──> Passive Triggers ──> Internal Cooldown Applied
```

### Passive Activation Architecture

| Component | UI Behavior | Trigger Type | Cooldown Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Token Counters** | Number overlay on hotbar icon | Collecting designated ability tokens | Resets to 0 upon activation |
| **Direct Procs** | Flashing status effect | Taking mob damage, getting hit by drops | Fixed timer before next trigger |
| **Consumable Procs** | Stacking counter | Using items (Gumdrops, Stingers) | Timer-based lockouts apply |
| **Permanent Auras** | Persistent status indicator | Continuous field presence | Always active (no cooldown) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective defensive Survive The Swarm passive for boss fights?
The *Emergency Coconut Shield* (granted by the Coconut Canister) is widely considered the top defensive passive. It provides 100% defense for 10 seconds and boosts bee attack power by 25% whenever you take direct mob damage, giving you ample time to eliminate threats.

### Can passive ability cooldowns be bypassed by unequipping items?
No. Equipping an accessory with an active passive applies an initial lockout timer equal to its standard cooldown. This mechanic prevents players from rapidly swapping gear to bypass cooldown restrictions.

### How does Star Saw help me survive dangerous mob swarms?
*Star Saw* summons a high-speed rotating blade around your player model for 30 seconds after using 3 Stingers. It continuously damages nearby hostile mobs based on your hive's attack power and level while instantly harvesting ability tokens and converting pollen.

### Do passive effects scale with Gifted bee types?
Yes. Many passives scale directly with Gifted bees. For instance, *Diamond Drain* increases its conversion power based on your Gifted Blue bee types, while *Fuzzy Coat* upgrades up to 9 flowers simultaneously when Fuzzy Bee is Gifted.
