## Threat Tiers: Categorizing Survive The Swarm Enemies

The insectoid invasion comprises distinct archetypes designed to exploit different squad vulnerabilities. Knowing which targets to burst down first prevents your exterminators from becoming overwhelmed.

```
                    +--------------------------------+
                    |        Stage Bosses            |  <-- High HP, Bullet-Hell Attacks
                    +---------------+----------------+
                                    |
                    +---------------+----------------+
                    |        Elite Beetles           |  <-- Modifiers, Heavy Armor
                    +---------------+----------------+
                                    |
                    +---------------+----------------+
                    |     Skirmishers & Spiders      |  <-- Speed, Leap, Acid Projectiles
                    +---------------+----------------+
                                    |
                    +---------------+----------------+
                    |      Drone Swarms (Fodder)     |  <-- Screen Congestion, Body Blocks
                    +--------------------------------+
```

### 1. Swarm Fodder (Drones & Creepers)
These represent the basic units that flood the screen. While individually weak, they serve two purposes: blocking your squad's movement path and body-blocking high-priority targets. Letting them accumulate creates suffocating killboxes.

### 2. Fast Harassers (Leapers & Skirmishers)
Harassers break standard movement patterns. They dodge ranged fire, leap directly into your backline, or flank your squad while you target larger foes. Heroes with Ice or Lightning powers are essential to pin them down before they chip away your team's health pool.

### 3. Armored Bruisers & Ranged Spitters
Armored beetles feature thick chitin plating that significantly reduces non-critical physical damage. Meanwhile, ranged spitters stay along screen edges, launching acidic mortar shots or straight-line bullet patterns that force you out of safe zones.

| Enemy Category | Threat Rating | Primary Attack Type | Best Direct Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fodder Drones** | Low | Direct contact / Swarm density | Fire AoE, Hammer sweeps |
| **Skirmisher Leapers** | Moderate | Fast leaps, rapid claw swipes | Ice Slows, Lightning chains |
| **Acid Spitters** | High | Long-range parabolic acid pools | Precision Longbow (Blaze), Dash burst |
| **Armored Beetles** | High | Charge attacks, damage reflection | Poison DoT, Armor-shred abilities |
| **Quest Mini-Bosses** | Very High | Summoning waves, telegraphed slams | Coordinated squad ultimates |

Handling diverse waves of **Survive The Swarm enemies** requires tailoring weapon enhancements mid-match to address current threats rather than spamming a single damage type.

## Boss and Elite Mechanics Breakdown

Every run features timed Swarm Events and climactic boss battles.         |                                                                                      |

### Elite Beetle Variants
Elite enemies appear highlighted with distinct elemental auras and modifier mechanics:
* **Haste Modifiers:** Greatly increase swarm movement speed, requiring immediate crowd control.
* **Shielding Shells:** Create barrier shields protecting surrounding lesser bugs from incoming projectiles.
* **Mortar Pods:** Continuously rain acid projectiles across your escape paths.

### Stage Boss Fight Strategies
Stage bosses combine bullet-hell bullet curtains with massive area-of-effect ground slams. 

1. **Watch the Ground Telegraphs:** Ground markers appear 1.5 seconds before heavy slams. Save your squad dashes exclusively for dodging these rings.
2. **Clear the Reinforcements First:** Bosses regularly summon waves of **Survive The Swarm enemies** to distract your auto-targeting weapons. Use wide-cleave heroes like Isla to maintain a clear line of sight to the boss.
3. **Exploit the Vulnerability Phase:** After executing an ultimate attack, bosses briefly enter a recovery state where their defense drops. Trigger your squad's highest single-target active abilities during this opening.

| Boss Phase Stage | Attack Pattern | Threat Mechanics | Response Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1 (100%–70% HP)** | Radial Bullet Rings, Basic Charges | Basic directional attacks | Circle-strafe while maintaining mid-range |
| **Stage 2 (70%–30% HP)** | Acid Mortar Carpet, Drone Summons | High zoning hazard, adds blocking path | Clear adds with Fire/Ice; hold position center |
| **Enrage (30%–0% HP)** | Non-stop charges, shockwave pulses | Arena-wide hazard density | Burn down remaining HP using burst cooldowns |

```
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                  ELEMENTAL ADVANTAGE MATRIX                 |
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
       | Fire      ---> Burn DoT / Armor Degradation                 |
       | Ice       ---> Freeze / Movement Speed Reduction            |
       | Lightning ---> Chain Arcs / High Critical Burst             |
```

### Optimizing Team Composition
* **Frontline Control (e.g., Isla's Hammer):** Sweeps away incoming fodder swarms, creating space for ranged teammates.
* **Precision Backline (e.g., Blaze's Longbow):** Takes down elite spitters and targets boss weak points from a distance.
* **Elemental Pairing:** Combine Ice abilities to slow large packs, then ignite them with Fire to trigger explosive status reactions that clear full screens.

| Elemental Element | Core Status Effect | Best Against Enemy Type | Recommended Hero Archetype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Essential Combat Strategies for Defeating The Swarm

To maximize your 15-minute survival runs and optimize Honor Point yields, apply these battle-tested combat tactics:

```
Combat Decision Tree:
Are you surrounded?
 ├── YES --> Trigger Ice slow / Dash through thinnest line of fodder --> Re-cluster bugs
 └── NO  --> Focus fire on Elite/Spitters --> Maintain circular kiting path
```

### 1. Master the Funnel Kite
Never run in straight lines across the arena. Moving in wide, gentle parabolas pulls **Survive The Swarm enemies** into a dense tail behind your squad. This allows piercing weapons and AoE spells to maximize their damage per second (DPS).

### 2. Prioritize Ranged Spitters
When an elite wave spawns, resist the urge to tunnel-vision on the closest melee bugs. Dash laterally to eliminate ranged acid spitters immediately, as their lingering damage pools severely limit your squad's safe zones.

### 3. Balance Meta-Progression
Honor Points accumulated across victories and defeats should be spent methodically. Prioritizing persistent squad upgrades like movement speed and cooldown reduction provides the baseline stats needed to consistently outmaneuver end-game **Survive The Swarm enemies**.

| Strategic Priority | Early Game (Runs 1–5) | Mid Game (Runs 6–15) | Late Game (Runs 16+) |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most dangerous Survive The Swarm enemies to watch out for?
Ranged acid spitters and fast leapers represent the highest immediate threats. While fodder swarms look intimidating due to screen density, ranged spitters restrict movement space, leaving your squad vulnerable to lethal boss attacks or elite charges.

### How do I effectively counter armored elite bugs?
Armored elites possess heavy defense against flat physical strikes. Use Poison-based exterminators to apply stacking percentage health drain, or combine Ice freeze effects with Fire damage to break their armor plating.

### What should I do during timed Quest Events when swarmed?
Focus on crowd control over raw single-target damage. Deploy Ice and Fire heroes to form a defensive perimeter around your objectives (such as solar panels). Avoid chasing stray elites away from the quest zone to ensure timers do not expire.

### How does enemy scaling work throughout a 15-minute run?
**Survive The Swarm enemies** scale in overall health, movement speed, and density at key phase transitions (roughly every 3 to 4 minutes). Keeping up with this scaling requires actively clearing quest capsules, collecting upgrade crates, and unlocking mid-run squad evolutions.
