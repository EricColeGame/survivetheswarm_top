## How Evolution Missions and Upgrades Work

In the Heart of the Swarm campaign, evolution missions serve as the ultimate proving ground for your army's genetic potential. Guided by the master evolver Abathur aboard the Leviathan, these optional scenarios allow you to test two competing strains in simulated live-combat environments.

Once an evolution mission is complete, your choice is permanent for that unit strain throughout the remainder of the campaign. Unlike standard mutations—which can be swapped freely between missions in the Evolution Pit—strain evolutions fundamentally alter how a unit functions.

```
Campaign Progression ➔ Unlock Evolution Mission ➔ Test Both Strains ➔ Select Permanent Evolution
```

### Green vs. Purple Strain Philosophies

Blizzard designed the evolution options around two distinct visual and strategic archetypes:

*   **Green Strains (Aggression & Area Damage):** These adaptations emphasize raw damage output, additional summoned units, or devastating siege capabilities to break fortifications quickly.
*   **Purple Strains (Sustain & Tactical Utility):** These variants focus on survival mechanisms, mobility leaps, crowd control, and instant battlefield replenishment.

## Early Game: Core Infantry Strains

The foundation of your army relies on low-tier, high-volume units that absorb damage and clear initial enemy waves.

```
       ┌── Raptor (Cliffs, +2 Dmg)
Zergling
       └── Swarmling (3/egg, 2s Spawn)

       ┌── Splitter (Acid Spawns)
Baneling
       └── Hunter (Cliff Jumper)
```

### Zergling: Raptor vs. Swarmling

The Zergling is your primary frontline fighter. The choice here defines whether you rely on sudden burst lethality or endless reinforcement waves.

| Attribute | Raptor Strain | Swarmling Strain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Color Archetype** | Green (Offensive) | Purple (Sustain/Swarm) |
| **Special Trait** | Cliff jumping, leaps to close distance | Spawns 3 Zerglings per egg |
| **Combat Bonus** | +2 base damage (+40% increase) | Spawns almost instantly (2 sec) |
| **Best Used For** | Flanking siege lines, burst assassination | Immediate base defense, endless swarming |
| **Recommended Mutation** | Hardened Carapace or Shredding Claws | Metabolic Boost |

*Player experience indicates that Raptor Zerglings provide unmatched mobility on maps with heavy elevation shifts, while Swarmlings excel in missions requiring sudden base defense.*

### Baneling: Splitter vs. Hunter

Rolling explosives clear light bio units and dense defensive clusters.

| Trait | Splitter Strain | Hunter Strain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Color Archetype** | Green | Purple |
| **Mechanic** | Splits into mini-banelings upon death | Jumps up and down cliffs; leaps at foes |
| **Secondary Effect** | Mini-spawns deal 5 damage each | Bypasses frontline tanks directly to backline |
| **Ideal Target** | Massive clusters of bio infantry | High-value siege targets on high ground |

Choosing between these builds depends on map design. If you need to hit entrenched Terran siege tanks perched on cliffs, the Hunter strain solves line-of-sight challenges immediately.

## Mid Game: Mid-Tier Armored Adaptations

As enemy forces deploy mechanized and armored battalions, evolving your Roaches and Hydralisks becomes essential to maintaining frontline stability.

```
       ┌── Corpser (Spawns Roachlings)
Roach ─┤
       └── Vile (Attack/Move Slow)

          ┌── Impaler (Burrowed Anti-Armor)
Hydralisk ┤
          └── Lurker (Burrowed Line AoE)
```

### Roach: Corpser vs. Vile

The Roach acts as your primary damage sponge. Evolving them properly dictates how much incoming fire your backline avoids.

| Feature | Corpser Strain | Vile Strain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Meat shield generation | Massive crowd control / Debuffing |
| **Trigger** | Killing an enemy unit tagged by Roach | Any basic attack landing on a target |
| **Effect** | Spawns temporary Roachling minions | Substantially slows enemy movement & attack speed |
| **Synergy** | High-damage support units behind | Hero-focused battles, anti-mech engagements |

The Vile Roach is widely considered one of the strongest **Survive The Swarm evolutions** across high-difficulty campaign runs because its slowing debuff cripples high-damage enemy threats like Battlecruisers, Thors, and Ultralisks.

### Hydralisk: Impaler vs. Lurker

Hydralisks transform your mid-tier forces into specialized siege specialists.

| Evolution | Primary Target Type | Attack Style | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Impaler** | Heavy Armored (Mech, Colossi) | Long-range single-target spike (Burrowed) | Smashing defensive structures and heavy tanks |
| **Lurker** | Light Armored (Marines, Zealots) | Linear area-of-effect spine wave (Burrowed) | Chokepoint defense and wave clearance |

*   **Carrion Strain:** Spawns flying locusts that bypass terrain to assault ground units. They deal 50% more damage with 25% less health, drastically increasing your forward burst damage.
*   **Creeper Strain:** Generates automatic creep wherever it burrows and gains the **Deep Tunnel** ability, allowing near-instant map-wide repositioning to any creep tile.

### Ultralisk: Noxious vs. Torrasque

The Ultralisk serves as the Swarm's ultimate apex predator.

| Metric | Noxious Strain | Torrasque Strain |

| **Primary Strength** | Liquefies infantry deathballs instantly | Unstoppable sustained front-line tanking |

According to community reports from speedrunners and Brutal-difficulty players, the Torrasque strain offers unprecedented value by negating supply losses during massive enemy counter-attacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my strain choices later in the campaign?
No. Unlike standard mutations in the Evolution Pit—which you can swap between missions at will—strain evolutions are permanent commitments once you finish their specific evolution mission. Choose the adaptation that best fits your broader strategic plan.

### Which are the most effective Survive The Swarm evolutions for Brutal difficulty?
On Brutal difficulty, crowd control and unit conservation are paramount. The most reliable **Survive The Swarm evolutions** for high difficulty include the **Vile Roach** for its massive attack-speed slow, the **Torrasque Ultralisk** for free resurrection mechanics, and the **Raptor Zergling** for bypassing chokepoints quickly.

### Does Kerrigan's ability loadout impact which evolutions I should pick?
Yes. If you run Kerrigan with energy-heavy offensive abilities like *Kinetic Blast* and *Crushing Grip*, choosing passive, durable strains like Vile Roaches and Torrasque Ultralisks allows her to cast freely behind a durable frontline. If you build Kerrigan as a support healer or economy booster, offensive strains like Raptors and Carrion Swarm Hosts thrive under her buffs.
