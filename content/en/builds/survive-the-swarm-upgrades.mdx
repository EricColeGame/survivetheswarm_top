### Persistent Upgrades (Honor Points)
Regardless of whether your squad survives the entire 15-minute timer or falls early, every run rewards you with **Honor Points**. These points are spent between matches to unlock global stat boosts that apply to all current and future squad members. 

Key benefits of persistent progression include:
- Squad-wide flat and percentage damage increases
- Enhanced base movement speed and dash recovery
- Ability cooldown reduction for active skills
- Increased defensive survivability and pickup ranges

### Mid-Run Tactical Upgrades
During an active match, exterminators gain experience as bugs are cleared, leveling up to unlock new ability tiers and modifier cards. Additionally, map interactions—such as completing timed Quest Events, defending solar panels, and securing reward capsules—grant temporary power spikes tailored to that specific run.

| Upgrade Type | Currency / Source | Scope | Persistence | Primary Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta-Upgrades** | Honor Points | Entire Account / Squad | Permanent | Base stats, cooldowns, speed |
| **Unit Level-Ups** | Experience (XP Orbs) | Individual Character | Run Only | Skill evolutions, passives |
| **Quest Rewards** | Timed Objectives | Active Squad | Run Only | Stat bonuses, reward capsules |
| **Roster Recruits** | Mid-Run Recruit Zones | Team Composition | Run Only | Adding new elemental units |

## 15-Minute Run Strategy & Quest Routing

Navigating the structured 15-minute timer requires a clear roadmap. Allocating your squad's time efficiently ensures you never fall behind the enemy stat curve.

```
0:00 ─────────────► 3:00 ─────────────► 8:00 ─────────────► 12:00 ────────────► 15:00
 Phase 1: Scouting    Phase 2: Roster      Phase 3: Mid-Run     Phase 4: Boss Prep   Final Boss
 & XP Farming        Expansion & Quests   Swarm Defense        & Capsule Looting    Showdown
```

### Phase-by-Phase Match Breakdown

1. **Minutes 0:00 – 3:00 (Early Clear & XP Harvesting)**: 
   Focus on tight circular kiting. Collect all green/blue XP gems to secure your starting exterminator's first three ability upgrades before the initial elite spawns.

2. **Minutes 3:00 – 8:00 (Recruitment & Quest Objectives)**: 
   Head straight toward Recruit Events to expand your team to 3 or 4 units. Complete timed quests—such as protecting solar collectors—to unlock high-tier squad bonus capsules.

3. **Minutes 8:00 – 12:00 (Surge Defense & Synergy Capping)**: 
   Insect density peaks here. Funnel the horde into environmental choke points. Ensure all squad members have their primary elemental synergies online.

4. **Minutes 12:00 – 15:00 (Boss Preparation & Final Push)**: 
   Clear remaining elite insects, grab leftover stat pickups, and coordinate your active cooldowns for the final boss showdown.

| Match Phase | Time Window | Primary Objective | Upgrades Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Setup** | 0:00 – 3:00 | Harvest early XP, scout map | Lead weapon damage & range |
| **Phase 2: Expansion** | 3:00 – 8:00 | Complete quests, recruit units | Unlock elemental passives |
| **Phase 3: Swarm Peak** | 8:00 – 12:00 | Hold choke points, survive waves | Max AoE skills & cooldowns |
| **Phase 4: Climax** | 12:00 – 15:00 | Defeat final insect boss | Single-target DPS & survivability |

## 5 Pro Tips for Optimizing Your Upgrades

- **Balance Single-Target and AoE**: Do not build four pure area-of-effect units. You need dedicated single-target burst (like Blaze's longbow) to drop boss health bars quickly.
- **Never Ignore Movement Speed Upgrades**: Being 10% faster than the swarm prevents chip damage and lets you safely reposition around elite charging bugs.
- **Focus Honor on Universal Multipliers**: Early in meta-progression, invest Honor Points into flat damage and cooldowns rather than hyper-niche unlocks.
- **Complete Every Timed Quest**: Quests reward high-rarity upgrade capsules that grant stat boosts otherwise unobtainable through standard level-ups.
- **Match Elements to Squad Strengths**: Always check the elemental alignment of recruited exterminators to ensure their skills trigger combo effects with your existing team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most important Survive The Swarm upgrades for beginners?
Beginners should invest their first batches of Honor Points into Squad Movement Speed and Global Damage. Increased movement speed lets you evade swarms easily, while higher base damage prevents early-game waves from overwhelming your starting unit.

### How do I earn Honor Points for meta-progression?
You earn Honor Points by completing match phases, defeating elite bugs, finishing timed quests, and surviving as long as possible during a 15-minute run. Honor Points are awarded at the end of every match regardless of whether you win or lose.

### Can I reset or respec my Honor Point upgrades?
Yes, you can reallocate your spent Honor Points between runs in the main hub menu, allowing you to experiment freely with different meta-upgrade setups and stat builds.

### How do mid-run character evolutions work?
As exterminators collect XP orbs dropped by defeated bugs, they level up individually. Each level lets you pick from randomized upgrade cards that boost weapon damage, expand elemental effects, or unlock advanced active ability modifiers.
