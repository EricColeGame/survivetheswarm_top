## Overview of Survive The Swarm Classes and Playstyles

Every class in *Survive The Swarm* brings a distinct set of base stats, scaling multipliers, and weapon affinities to the battlefield. Progression revolves around clearing waves, gathering **Gold** to purchase weapons and gear, and spending valuable **Essence** to unlock deeper class passives and evolutions. 

According to community reports and early player testing, classes generally fit into four primary combat archetypes:

- **Melee Brawlers:** High base HP, damage mitigation, and cleave attacks suited for frontline wave control.
- **Ranged Marksmen:** High single-target DPS, long reach, and critical strike scaling designed to melt elite foes.
- **Arcane Mages:** Wide-range area-of-effect (AoE) abilities that excel at clearing dense mob swarms quickly.
- **Hybrid Support / Tacticians:** Focus on sustain, mobility, crowd control, and squad-wide buffing.

## Strategy: Dominating the 3-Hour Lobby Raid Boss

One of the standout features of the game is the massive lobby raid boss that arrives on a strict three-hour timer. Because the player dealing the highest total damage walks away with exclusive weapons and premium Essence caches, your class choice and battle preparation are critical.

Follow these proven steps during raid boss encounters:

1. **Pre-Buff in the Lobby:** Ensure your Gold-based consumables (Damage Flasks and Speed Elixirs) are active before the boss spawn countdown hits zero.
2. **Prioritize Armor Shred:** If running a duo or squad, have one player apply defense-reducing debuffs so single-target classes (like the Shadow Ranger) deal amplified burst damage.
3. **Positioning Over Greed:** The boss features wide-area telegraphs. Dodging attacks keeps your DPS uptime higher than dying and waiting through respawn penalties.
4. **Burst Phase Synchronization:** Save your class ultimate abilities for when the boss enters its stunned or staggered state to trigger maximum critical hit multipliers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Survive The Swarm classes for solo players?
For solo play, the **Elementalist Mage** and **Frontline Berserker** are top recommendations. The Elementalist provides unparalleled wave-clearing speed for rapid resource accumulation, while the Berserker offers built-in lifesteal and defensive stats that forgive positioning mistakes during intense horde waves.

### How do I unlock new classes in Survive The Swarm?
New classes and class evolutions are unlocked by spending **Essence** in the main hub character menu or by reaching specific wave survival milestones. You can also acquire bonus starting Essence and Gold through developer promo codes and raid boss clears.

### Can I reset my class upgrades and stat points?
Yes. Players can respec their class trees in the lobby hub. While some respec options require a nominal Gold fee, doing so lets you freely adapt your build when transitioning from mob farming to raid boss battles.

### How does Essence differ from Gold when upgrading classes?
Gold is used for basic gear, stat boosts, and weapon purchases during individual runs. Essence functions as a permanent meta-progression currency required to unlock core class archetypes, deep passive skill trees, and evolved weapon tiers.
