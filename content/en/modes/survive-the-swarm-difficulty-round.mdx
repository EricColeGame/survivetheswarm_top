## Understanding the Core Game Modes and Difficulty Mechanics

The game offers several distinct playstyles tailored to different levels of challenge and player preferences. Players can engage in randomized Expeditions, standard Skirmishes, or jump into the grueling Endless mode. Across these modes, the difficulty system—often referred to in community discussions as Infestation tiers (scaling up to Infestation 5)—modifies enemy density, movement speed, harassment frequency, and attack power.

Community reports and player experiences shared on the [Steam Community review hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3052730/reviews/) highlight that while standard modes offer approachable onboarding, higher difficulty settings severely punish inefficient build orders and neglected map exploration.

Community veterans who consistently break the round 30 barrier emphasize that base layout ("town planning") and proactive fortification upgrades are non-negotiable.

## Step-by-Step Strategic Checklist for Tough Waves

To consistently clear every difficult swarm encounter, follow this actionable protocol from game start to late-stage defenses.

1. **Secure Choke Points Early:** Scout immediate terrain features during the first 2 minutes. Identify narrow passages between forests and rock faces to minimize wall costs.
2. **Establish Redundant Resource Loops:** Never rely on a single cluster of woodcutters or stone quarries. Harassment units can sever single-node incomes, stalling your production.
3. **Utilize Active Pause Frequently:** Do not hesitate to pause the game during heavy micro-management moments. Queue unit movements, assign repair orders, and adjust tower target priorities calmly.
4. **Prepare Secondary Fallback Lines:** When facing a high-level **Survive The Swarm difficulty round**, design dual-layer defenses. If your first gate falls, retreat units behind your secondary barricade while ballistas maintain continuous fire.
5. **Manage Attrition and Salvage:** If upkeep costs outpace resource generation after a devastating wave, quickly dismantle damaged, non-essential buildings to recover resources and stabilize population limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes a high Survive The Swarm difficulty round harder to manage?
As difficulty rounds advance, swarm frequency increases, intervals between waves decrease, and harassment packs spawn more often. Additionally, enemy health pools and resistances scale significantly, requiring optimized Deity Essence synergies and advanced tower tech.

### How often should I use the active pause feature?
Active pause is essential whenever you are managing multiple fronts simultaneously, planning new building grids, or responding to unexpected flanking harassment forces. Using pause prevents unnecessary unit losses during chaotic micro-management phases.

### Why do subsequent swarms arrive faster than the initial 10-minute wave?
The first 10 minutes serve as an introductory grace period to establish foundational resource extraction and basic defenses. Subsequent swarms compress down to roughly 3- to 5-minute windows (depending on your chosen Infestation tier) to test your economic scaling under pressure.

### How do I survive harassment units between main wave intervals?
Keep dedicated small patrol squads of ranged units near vulnerable resource extractors, or wall off peripheral economic hubs with light barricades and watchtowers to repel wanderers without diverting your main strike force.
