## Core Mechanics of the 15-Minute Survival Run

Each survival session runs on a strict 15-minute timer divided into specific phase intervals. Surviving the clock requires planning your route around timed objectives rather than wandering aimlessly.

```
00:00 ── Start Run ──► 03:00 Quests ──► 06:00 Recruits ──► 10:00 Swarms ──► 15:00 Boss Extraction
```

The battlefield constantly shifts as new objectives appear. Efficient players prioritize building structures like solar panels within tight time limits, clearing elite targets, and cracking open reward capsules before the next swarm wave crashes into the team.

| Run Phase | Active Timer | Primary Objective | Key Threat | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Recon** | 0:00 – 3:00 | Secure initial XP, level starter unit | Fast swarmer scouts | Focus on tight kiting and basic clear |
| **Quest Inception** | 3:00 – 6:00 | Complete timed solar panel & elite tasks | Elite bug spawns | Group enemies into choke points |
| **Mid-Game Reinforcement** | 6:00 – 10:00 | Trigger recruit events & expand roster | Armored hive clusters | Build elemental synergies (e.g., Ice + Lightning) |
| **Swarm Surge** | 10:00 – 14:00 | Survive dense multi-angle assaults | Elite mini-boss waves | Rotate squad dashes, prioritize area control |
| **Final Boss Climax** | 14:00 – 15:00 | Eliminate final boss & secure extraction | High-damage boss patterns | Focus burst damage, dodge heavy area attacks |

### Balancing Kiting with Quest Objectives

To claim maximum rewards in a run, you must multitask. Ignoring quests leaves your squad underpowered for the late-game waves. Keep moving constantly while drawing insect groups toward quest objectives so collateral area-of-effect (AoE) damage chips down objectives and waves simultaneously.

Before the 10-minute swarm surge, recruit a dedicated single-target specialist like Blaze. High crit scaling and focused projectile weapons ensure you melt end-of-run bosses before they overwhelm your defensive dashes.

## Advanced Tactics to Complete the Swarm Challenge

Executing a clean 15-minute victory requires mastering map movement and unit ability timing. Use these battle-tested strategies to gain an upper hand:

- **Perimeter Looping:** Avoid running directly into open territory when swarms surround you. Circle the edges of the active swarm to herd bugs into tight clusters, then detonate them with elemental reactions.
- **Save Dashes for Telegraphs:** Never use squad dashes simply to move faster across empty ground. Keep charges ready to escape elite bug leap attacks and boss slam radiuses.
- **Quest-Priority Farming:** Always prioritize solar panel construction when the timer starts. Completing every quest in a single run is mandatory if you want to claim high-tier achievements like "Upstanding Citizen."
- **Stat Card Verification:** When evaluating weapon drops (such as the Mitosic Arrow), check both critical chance and flat attack power values to properly balance sustained DoT against burst damage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Survive The Swarm challenge?
The **Survive The Swarm challenge** is the core gameplay mode in *Survive the Swarm*, where players guide an evolving squad of solarpunk exterminators through 15 minutes of escalating insect attacks, dynamic quest events, and elite boss battles.

### How do I earn Honor Points fast?
Honor Points are awarded at the end of every run based on insect kills, completed timed quests, elites defeated, and total survival time. Maximizing quest completions and eliminating elite waves yields the highest point totals.

### Which elemental synergy is best for beginners?
The Fire and Poison combination (*Combustion Cloud*) is easiest for beginners because it provides massive passive damage-over-time and clears screen-filling swarms with minimal aiming required.

### Can I play the game on handheld devices like the Steam Deck?
Yes. The game runs smoothly at 60 FPS on handheld setups. Ensure your control layout maps UI confirmation and dashes comfortably to gamepad buttons for fast-paced dodging.
